Deposit insurance limit

The standard FDIC deposit insurance amount is $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category. Coverage can be higher when ownership categories differ. This page does not calculate your coverage.

FDIC explanation of deposit insurance

What these numbers mean

Deposits are the balances customers and others have at the bank, as reported on the call report. Assets are everything the bank reports owning. Domestic offices come from the FDIC institution record and include the main office.

Total risk-based capital ratio is the FDIC field for total risk-based capital divided by risk-weighted assets. Common equity tier 1 capital ratio and the tier 1 leverage ratio are separate FDIC capital measures. Equity to assets is total equity capital divided by total assets, computed on this site from those two dollar amounts. None of these is a grade.

Noncurrent loans are loans and leases 90 days or more past due, or in nonaccrual, as a percent of gross loans and leases. A higher percent means a larger share of the loan book is in that status. It is not a forecast. Return on assets is annualized net income as a percent of average total assets.

Percentiles, when shown, count how many other active banks reported a lower figure for the same quarter. The method is on the methodology page.
