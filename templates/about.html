About the Bank Safety & Savings Board

A free, no-login lookup of FDIC-insured banks and federally insured credit unions. Kenneth Davis runs it independently. It is not a bank, credit union, broker, or adviser, and it is not part of the FDIC or the NCUA.

What the pages do

Each active bank and each federally insured credit union has its own page of figures from the latest public call report: insurance status, size, capital or net worth, problem loans, and earnings. The figures are the agencies’ numbers. The site does not turn them into a score.

Not financial advice. Nothing here recommends opening, keeping, or closing an account. Reports can be revised, and a strong ratio in one quarter does not decide what happens next. Confirm anything important on the linked FDIC or NCUA record.
