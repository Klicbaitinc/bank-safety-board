What these numbers mean

Shares and deposits are member shares and other deposits on the call report. Assets are total assets. Net worth ratio on the NCUA list is net worth divided by total assets, excluding the CECL transition provision. The trend column net worth ÷ assets uses the call-report dollar amounts for total net worth and total assets, so it can differ slightly from the list.

Delinquent loans are loans and leases that are two months or more past due, divided by total loans and leases. That is a 60-day measure. A bank’s noncurrent-loan ratio is a different statistic (90 days or nonaccrual). Return on average assets is the figure published on the NCUA insured-credit-union list for this quarter.

Branch offices are rows marked “Branch Office” in the NCUA branch file. The corporate office is counted separately.
